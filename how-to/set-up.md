# How to set it up

## How your instructions fit in

Every chatbot reads several layers of instructions before it answers. You control one of them.

```
1. The company's system prompt     ← you can't change this
2. An app maker's instructions     ← e.g. a school AI tool; ask what it says
3. YOUR instructions               ← custom instructions, a Gem, a Project
4. The chat itself                 ← what your child types
```

Your layer can change **how** the AI helps: ask first, give hints, push back. It can't override the company's safety rules. And the chat itself can wear your layer down: if a child keeps asking for the answer, some AIs will give in. That's why you should [test it](test-it.md).

You can see these layers in the [system prompts we collected](../what-the-ai-is-told/). For example, ChatGPT's ends with slots for "User Bio," "User's Instructions" and the user's stored memories.

## Where to paste it

Menu names change often. If you can't find a setting, search the app's help pages for the name in bold.

| App | Lasts for every chat | Lasts for one topic or tool | One chat only |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ChatGPT** | **Custom instructions** (in personalization settings) | A **Project** with instructions, or a **custom GPT** | Paste the [start-of-chat version](../prompts/kids-6-to-11.md#start-of-chat-version) as the first message |
| **Gemini** | **Saved info** | A **Gem** (a custom Gemini with its own instructions) | Same as above |
| **Claude** | **Personal preferences** (in profile settings) | A **Project** with instructions | Same as above |

**Which one to use:**
- **For a child's own account:** custom instructions or saved info, so every chat starts with them.
- **For a shared family device or a parent's account:** a Gem, Project or custom GPT named something like "Thinking Partner." Your child opens that one, and your own chats stay as they are.
- **For one time, on any device:** the start-of-chat version.

## Check the age rules first

Each company sets its own minimum age. Check the current terms, because they change.

| App | Minimum age (as of 2026) |
|---|---|
| ChatGPT | 13, with a parent's permission under 18. OpenAI also offers parental controls for linked teen accounts and a Study Mode. |
| Gemini | Under 13 only through a parent-managed Google account (Family Link). |
| Claude | 18. Anthropic's apps are not for children. |

A child under the minimum age should not have their own account. Many families sit with a younger child and use a parent's account together. If you do this, use the [start-of-chat version](../prompts/kids-6-to-11.md#start-of-chat-version) and stay nearby.

## Write it together

The kid and teen prompts are written in your child's voice. That's on purpose: it's their learning, and rules they helped write are rules they keep.

1. **Read it out loud together.** Stop at each rule and ask: why do you think this one is here?
2. **Let them change the words.** If a line doesn't sound like them, rewrite it. Keep the meaning.
3. **Stay close the first few times.** Listen to what they ask for and how the AI answers.
4. **Ask about it afterward.** "What did you figure out? What did it ask you?" This helps them notice how they use it.

---

## What this can't do

- **It isn't a lock.** A child who controls the account can delete your instructions. A child can also open a different app. These prompts work best as a default you agree on together.
- **It isn't parental controls.** It doesn't filter content, limit time or show you the chats. Use the app's own family settings for that.
- **It doesn't replace you.** The most useful thing you can do is talk with your child about what they're learning, with no screen involved.
